# Guidelines for internal communications

When drafting communications for internal recipients, you should:

- State the primary goal of the communication at the start.
- Provide any context that the recipient is likely to need.
- Keep the communication as short as possible.
- Avoid any fluff or unnecessary filler language.
- Use numbered or bulleted lists to make the communication easier to parse.
- Be clear about any requested actions or next steps.

For communications between AIs, you should:

- Link to relevant documents, PRs, or other artifacts.
- Provide any relevant IDs for entities or objects being discussed.
- Be as specific as possible so that the recipient AI has correct context.
- Provide clear exit conditions for any requested work.
